# Pre-trade checklist

Run this checklist before opening any position and on every run that manages open ones. If a check fails, do not open the trade: say which check failed and why, with the numbers. Answer in the language the person uses.

## Before opening a position

1. **News window.** Read the economic calendar (high impact) for the currencies behind the instrument (USD for gold, Nasdaq, S&P and BTC).
   - Fed rate decision and press conference: no new position from 60 minutes before to 90 minutes after.
   - CPI, PPI, NFP (jobs report), PCE, a speech by the Fed chair: 30 minutes before to 60 minutes after.
   - Any other high-impact event on that currency: 30 minutes before to 30 minutes after.
   Positions already open keep their stop and target through the event.
2. **Daily loss.** If today's realised loss plus open losses has reached the daily loss limit (3% of the morning balance when none is set), open nothing new today.
3. **Losing streak.** After 4 losing trades in a row on the same instrument, open nothing new on it until the next day.
4. **Exposure.** Never more than one position on the same instrument and direction unless the strategy explicitly allows more. Count trades that are the same bet (e.g. long gold and short USD/JPY are both "short USD"); total risk on one bet must stay at or under 3% of the balance.
5. **Size from the stop.** Size = risk amount ÷ distance to the stop, using the platform's contract size and lot step, rounded down. Default risk 1% of the balance. If the result is below the minimum size or the free margin is short, do not open: never enlarge the stop or the size to make an order fit.
6. **Spread.** If the current spread is more than 15% of the distance to the stop, wait: the trade starts too far behind.

## When opening

7. Stop loss and take profit go **in the opening order**. A market order without a stop is never sent.

## Every run with open positions

8. **Every position has a stop.** If one has none, set it now at the strategy's level, or at the 1% risk level when there is no strategy. If it cannot be set, close the position and report it.
9. **The stop only moves in the position's favour.** Never widen a stop, never remove a target to "let it run" unless the strategy says so.
10. Do not touch positions opened by someone else or by another agent, except to report a position without a stop.

## Journal

Every decision is logged in one line: time, instrument, which checks passed or failed, decision (open / hold / adjust / close / skip) and the reason.

This checklist protects the account; it does not predict the market. It is not financial advice.
